# Resonance

A quiet isometric journey for the phone about the voice of a singing bowl.

- **Make the bowl sing.** Circle the rim with your finger like a puja stick. A slow, steady circle builds a swelling tone. Go too fast and the stick chatters and the tone breaks. Tap the bowl to strike it.
- **Tune it with water.** More water lowers the pitch, from G4 (empty) down to about B3.
- **Listening stones** each hum a note. Near one, you hear the beating between your bowl and the stone. The wobble slows as you get in tune and disappears when you match it. Hold the note and the stone answers: a bridge rises, a lift carries you up, or two stones sing a chord together.
- Three chapters: The Listening Stone, Rising Water, Two Voices.
- All sound is synthesized live. The bowl is modelled partial by partial, with split modes for its natural wobble, a pulse from the stick going around the rim, wood friction, chatter and water gurgle. Generative pentatonic ambience, rain, wind and reverb. Works offline.
