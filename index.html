Clay of Bhaktapur

A feel prototype. Throw pots on a stick-spun wheel and fill the town's orders.

  1. Swipe across the wheel to spin it. It slows down, so keep it going.
  2. Hold a finger still on the clay to center it.
  3. Press into the top and drag down to open it.
  4. Drag the wall in or out to shape. Drag up at the rim to pull it taller.
  5. Keep it wet. Too thin, too tall or too wide and it collapses.

Sound on: turn off the silent switch.
