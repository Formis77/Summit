Pottery Square

Clay of Bhaktapur

Two fingers on the wall: one inside, one outside. Squeeze them together to thin the wall.
Slide both up to the rim. That's a pull: the squeezed clay rises and the pot grows taller.
Lean the pinch in or out while pulling to shape the belly and neck. Too thin or too wide and it collapses.

Sound on: turn off the silent switch.
